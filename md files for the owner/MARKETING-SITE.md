# Marketing Website Archetype

Use this file for landing pages, product marketing sites, company sites, portfolios, campaign pages, and other public-facing websites where storytelling and conversion are primary goals.

Combine with `BASE.md` when generating the project's root `DESIGN.md`.

---

## 1. Primary Goal

A marketing site should communicate value quickly and then guide the visitor through a deliberate narrative.

Typical priorities:

1. clarity of value proposition
2. brand and visual identity
3. information hierarchy
4. proof / credibility
5. conversion action
6. visual polish

---

## 2. Hero

The hero should answer quickly:

- what is this?
- who is it for?
- why does it matter?
- what should I do next?

Large typography is acceptable when it strengthens hierarchy.

Do not create a visually impressive hero that leaves the product unclear.

---

## 3. Section Rhythm

Use distinct visual chapters rather than a continuous wall of content.

Vary section composition intentionally while keeping the overall system consistent.

Whitespace can be more generous than in application UIs when it supports storytelling and comprehension.

---

## 4. Calls to Action

Establish a clear primary CTA hierarchy.

Avoid giving every button the same visual weight.

Repeat the primary CTA when useful, but do not make every section look like a sales block.

---

## 5. Typography

Typography can be more expressive than in dashboards or product interfaces, but readability and hierarchy still come first.

Large display type should be paired with restrained supporting text.

---

## 6. Imagery and Motion

Photography, illustration, video, and product imagery may carry more visual weight here than in applications.

Use motion to support storytelling and product comprehension.

Avoid decorative animation that overwhelms content or makes the site feel slow.

---

## 7. Product Demonstrations

When the site presents the product itself:

- simplify the product view to emphasize the message
- use realistic UI states
- avoid misleading mockups
- keep product screenshots consistent with the actual project design

---

## 8. Responsive Behavior

Marketing layouts often need compositional changes rather than simple stacking.

On mobile:

- preserve headline hierarchy
- shorten or reorder supporting content where necessary
- prioritize primary CTA
- avoid tiny product screenshots
- simplify decorative composition when it competes with content
