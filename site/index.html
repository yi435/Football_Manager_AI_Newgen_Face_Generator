Install in 3 steps ▾

  1. Download & run the installer. The wizard checks your system, downloads the AI engine and model, and links everything for you.
  2. Point it at FM. Pick your FM 2024 folder — the app watches for exports and writes faces into the game's graphics folder.
  3. Reload the skin. In FM: Preferences → Interface, untick "Use caching to decrease page loading times", then Clear Cache → Reload Skin.

In-game: open Player Search with the NewGen view/filter, select all (Ctrl+A), and press Ctrl+P → To text file. The app generates every new face automatically.

FAQ ▾

Why is the download ~9 GB on first run?

The small EXE installs a full offline AI engine (ComfyUI) plus the photorealistic RealVisXL model on your machine. It's a one-time download — after that everything is offline and free.

Can I generate while playing FM?

Yes. FM is mostly CPU-bound, and generation happens on your GPU. Each face takes ~5–15 seconds on an 8 GB card.

Do I need a GPU or cloud API key?

No cloud keys. You just need an NVIDIA GPU with at least 6–8 GB VRAM — generation runs 100% locally and offline.

Does it touch my real-player facepack?

No. NewGen faces are written to a separate graphics folder with their own config.xml, so existing facepacks are left untouched.
