What it does

Four things it handles for you, every time a new academy player appears in your save.

Realistic academy faces

Photorealistic headshots on a clean studio background — no logos, no grass, no waxy “AI” skin. Age, nationality and personality shape each portrait.

Auto-watch & generate

Point it at your exports folder once. The app watches it, spots new players, and generates every face automatically.

Consistent aging

Each player keeps the same facial structure as they grow. Hair, stubble and features update at ages 16, 20, 24 & 28.

Local & free forever

Everything runs on your GPU with a bundled offline engine. No cloud, no API keys, no subscription — your faces never leave your PC.

How it works

Steps tagged Auto are handled by the app. Manual ones need you, about a minute in FM.

  1. Auto
    One-click installer. Checks your system, downloads the engine (ComfyUI) and the RealVisXL model, and sets everything up with a progress bar.
  2. Manual
    Export your NewGens from FM. Apply the included filter, open Player Search, select all (Ctrl+A), then Ctrl+P → To text file. Save into the folder the app is watching.
  3. Auto
    Faces get generated. The app spots each new player, builds a picture-based prompt from their age/nationality/personality, and renders a face on your GPU.
  4. Manual
    Reload the skin in FM. Preferences → Interface, untick “Use caching”, then Clear Cache → Reload Skin. The faces appear in-game.

Quick start

The whole flow, in under five minutes.

Short version

1. Run the installer and let it finish the download + setup.

2. Export your NewGens to the watched folder (Ctrl+P → To text file).

3. Wait for the faces, then Reload Skin in FM settings.

The FM side: exporting players (exact clicks)

Open Player Search in FM24, apply the included filter, press Ctrl+A to select all rows, then Ctrl+P and choose “To text file”. Save it into the folder shown in the app — the app picks it up and generates every face.

⬇ Download NewGen export filter (.fmf)

GPU notes: which cards work well

A 6–8 GB card (e.g. RTX 2060/3060/4060) renders each face in roughly 5–15 seconds. Cards with less VRAM fall back to CPU mode, which is noticeably slower. Generation runs in the background, so you can keep playing while faces are made.

FAQ ▸

Why is the first download ~9 GB?

The small EXE installs a full offline AI engine (ComfyUI) plus the photorealistic RealVisXL model on your PC. One-time download — after that everything is offline and free.

It says “ComfyUI not connected” — what do I do?

The app auto-starts the engine and shows a green “Connected” badge once it’s ready. If it stays offline for over a minute, use Maintenance → Repair in the app — it fixes a broken engine or a misplaced model download automatically.

Do I need a cloud / API key?

No. No subscription, no credits, no keys. Generation runs 100% locally on your NVIDIA GPU.

Does it touch my real-player facepack?

No. NewGen faces are written to a separate graphics folder with their own config, so existing facepacks stay untouched.
